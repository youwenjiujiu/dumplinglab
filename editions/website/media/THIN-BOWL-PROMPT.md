# Less prominent vessel

Built-in imagegen produced this campaign variant. The reference edit retained a baked checkerboard and was rejected. A standalone transparent generation was accepted, retaining the same six century-egg wedges, noodles and chopped scallions, with a narrow rim rather than a deep visible bowl wall. This is a generated illustration, not a restaurant photograph.

Files: `hero-century-egg-noodles-v2.webp`, `hero-century-egg-noodles-v2-800.webp`. Version 1 is retained for comparison. Only the website campaign asset reference changed; menu photography and ordering data did not change.

## Final prompt

Use case: product-mockup
Create a real transparent PNG food cutout with true alpha channel, no background pixels. Standalone product photography asset.
Subject: Chinese century egg dry-tossed noodles, a large round mound of glossy golden amber noodles topped with exactly six generous century egg wedges (translucent dark amber whites and grey-green yolks) and chopped fresh green scallion rings. Ingredients are ONLY century egg, scallions and noodles.
Vessel is deliberately barely visible: an extremely shallow warm ivory dish with a delicate narrow rim, photographed from an elevated nearly top-down angle. No deep bowl wall, no broad empty ceramic surface, no bulky front vessel body. Food fills to the thin rim, covering at least 95% of visible subject area. Only a slender ivory outline supports the food; the bottom edge has no more than a 1% diameter sliver of ceramic. Do not shrink the food to make room for a bowl.
Premium photorealistic food photography, sharp edible texture, warm soft upper-left light. Complete round subject centered in square canvas with 8% transparent margin on every side. Real dimensional egg wedges and layered noodles, not flattened textures.
No spoon, chopsticks, prawns, dumplings, meat, parsley, soup, ground shadow, letters or logo. Background must be empty transparent alpha, NOT a printed checkerboard, NOT a white rectangle.
