# Full menu image coverage and preparation-first entry

## Flow

1. Choose a pictured preparation: broth / tossed / boiled / pan-fried.
2. Set preparation-specific options before continuing: spice and sesame sauce only for tossed; noodles +S$2.50 only for broth/tossed. Pan-fried adds S$1 per portion.
3. Explicitly continue to filling choice; no filling cards before this transition. Broth/tossed have 9 choices; boiled/pan-fried have 6, excluding the three restricted wonton items.
4. Choose filling, then 5/10 pieces in detail. Selected flavor carries through. Existing cart details retain their own saved variant.

Existing red/ivory design rules retained, with pictured entries and a clear selected-flavor/back action instead of adding decorative animation. Real-browser connection was unavailable; do not claim mobile visual QA.
